How it is calculated

Rain: NASA GPM IMERG V07
Half-hourly satellite rain summed by local day since 2001. Days not yet available are filled with NASA POWER and flagged.
Temperature and evapotranspiration: NASA POWER
Daily maximum and minimum, corrected for the parcel's elevation (6.5 °C per km), then Hargreaves reference evapotranspiration: how much water a well-watered crop would use.
Terrain: SRTM 30 m
Elevation for the temperature correction and slope. On slopes above 15%, soil protection weighs 1.5×.
Water Requirement Satisfaction Index (WRSI)
A daily soil-water bucket for each crop from the postrera onset to harvest. 100 means the crop got all the water it needed. Stress at flowering lowers the result one class, and so does heavy rain when beans are maturing.
Similar years
Past years are grouped by canícula length into short, normal and long thirds. The rotation statistics come only from years in the same group as this one.
What it does not know
Tempo supports, and does not replace, the judgment of farmers and local agronomists. Built for NASA Space Apps 2026, challenge Field Shift. Data: NASA GPM IMERG, NASA POWER, SRTM; soil from ISRIC SoilGrids.
