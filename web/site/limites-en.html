What this site cannot say

  • No real yields. There are no yearly harvest data for this plot; the score is a model, not an observed harvest.
  • The advantage depends on an assumption. Without the 150 mm bean rule, the margin over current practice is 0.00 and the pick changes in 25 of 25 years.
  • 2026 margin. The top option in 2026 leads the second by 0.003 points; the reading threshold is 0.05.
  • CHECK 1 not evaluable. 23 of 25 years give WRSI 100 for primera maize. With 60 mm water capacity, CHECK 1 does discriminate.
  • Weak signal. The mid-summer drought does not predict September–November rain (Spearman -0.21, 25 years).
  • Dependence on the rain source. With NASA POWER, the strategy changes in 8 of 25 years.
  • No risk light. It was removed: there are no yields and no drought check that would support a risk threshold.
  • Unvalidated assumptions. Water capacity per texture, crop coefficients, 0.6/0.4 land shares, priority weights, the 150 mm threshold and the reading threshold. All are ASSUMPTIONS.
  • One plot. Results do not generalise to other farms or the rest of the Dry Corridor.
  • Local validation pending. Review with CENTA technicians is needed before any field use.

Decision support for the postrera season. It does not replace local agronomic judgement. Data: NASA POWER, GPM IMERG V07, SRTM, ISRIC SoilGrids. Site built locally; not published.
