Method

How it is calculated

A simple daily water balance for the plot, with satellite rain and reanalysis temperature.

Steps

  1. Rain: IMERG V07 summed per local day (UTC−6) at the point. NASA POWER fills missing days.
  2. Temperature: NASA POWER, corrected for elevation with SRTM (-0.96 °C at this plot, −0.0065 °C/m).
  3. Evapotranspiration: Hargreaves with (Tmax+Tmin)/2, FAO-56 eq. 52.
  4. Soil: water capacity of 130 mm from the clayey texture (SoilGrids). The value per texture is an ASSUMPTION.
  5. Balance: daily bucket capped at capacity; stress starts below half of capacity. Simplified WRSI-type index, not full FAO-56. Classes: ≥ 80 good, 60–80 partial, < 60 failure; one class down for stress at flowering or, for beans, more than 150 mm of rain at the end of the cycle.
  6. Similar years: years are grouped into thirds by mid-summer drought length; each year uses the other years in the same third. Never the year being evaluated nor the current season.
  7. Ties: if two options tie on climate, current practice is kept. In the drought check, tied years take the highest rank, and if most years tie the check is marked “not evaluable”.
  8. Final score: 60 % climate and 40 % priorities. With slope above 15 % (here 19.9 %), the soil weight is multiplied by 1.5.
  9. Margin and sign test: the margin is the mean score difference against current practice. The sign test counts better and worse years and ignores ties; it does not account for assumptions shared across years.
